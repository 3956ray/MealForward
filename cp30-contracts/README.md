# CP30-S0b offline voucher fulfillment

DEVELOPER_COMPLETE_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW is recorded only after the evidence validator succeeds. This local slice implements the full frozen S0b lifecycle. It is not Leader acceptance, a deployed DApp, authentication, physical-delivery proof or finality evidence.

`ThreeStoreFactory` constructor-atomically creates exactly three unique fixed stores. Shared asset and quote authority and explicit per-store catalog, issuer and operator authorities are immutable; owner is the fixed settlement receiver. Catalog authority alone publishes, lists, pauses/resumes and irreversibly closes. Issuer issues/safely expires; operator locks, marks chain-time handover and reports; owner alone settles H; original payer alone refunds A. There is no admin, owner catalog fallback, fourth registration, role rotation, upgrade, asset/receiver setter, withdrawal/rescue, unlock or reopen.

Every original unit keeps its zero-based global sequence, batch/local index, payer, meal/version, computed hash and price. Ten lifetime funded units never reopen. Amounts and counts independently partition A/R/H/S/X; F=A+R+H+S+X, liability=A+R+H=F-S-X. Donation is surplus only. Incoming and outgoing SafeERC20 transfers require exact deltas at both endpoints. A single reentrancy guard covers every mutating entry point.

TermsInput is the frozen ten-field static tuple; constructor CatalogEntry wraps it and a current boolean. Initial current is unique/highest per meal and initially listed. This is an intentional constructor ABI change from C0, not an upgrade of an existing deployed escrow. Terms hashes bind schema1, chain, escrow, store, meal/version, asset, price, body/image digests, windows and UTC offset480 in the exact MealForwardTerms schema. Publication appends current+1, preserves old records/listing/closed/paused, and leaves newly published meals unlisted. The publisher fixture uses exact UTF8/LF and rejects CR; the contract validates nonzero commitments/rules, not content availability or image provenance.

Serving windows are daily UTC+8, exact-second [startMinute*60,endMinute*60), one/two sorted nonoverlapping windows, adjacency allowed, end1440 is next midnight. Original paid versions stay authoritative. Allocation scans at most ten original units for the lowest eligible same-meal A or safely expired unlocked/unmarked R. Issue normalizes safe expiries then compares the expected sequence/generation/hash atomically; mismatch rolls back normalization. Its original timestamp is execution block time and its exact expiry is +259200 seconds, with a bounded four-day/two-window intersection check. Fresh issue, lock and marker deadlines and voucher expiry are exclusive. Historical lookup never extends clocks or restores a right.

Lock is unique and remains R. Handover marker requires that lock under original time/window and records immutable block timestamp/number; no caller time is accepted. Report consumes only a lawful stored marker R→H and may be late. Owner settlement H→S pays original price. Explicit safe expiry only returns expired unlocked/unmarked R→A; locked R has no timeout release. Refund masks are batch-local; every bit must be A. Legacy refund shares the same implementation but repeated consumption rejects.

Fresh fund/issue reject unlisted/closed/paused. Old original-window lock/marker remain possible when unlisted/closed; paused rejects new lock/marker. Lawful report, H settlement, safe expiry/refund preserve old obligations under all controls; payouts still require coverage. Insolvency/balance-read faults reject fresh fund/issue/lock/marker and payouts; report/safe expiry can reclassify existing liability without restoring solvency. Resume never reopens closed or extends expiry/cap.

Funding retains C0's exact EIP712 domain/type, payer, valid signature and successful quote lookup. Control/lifecycle operation kinds1..11 use the frozen operation payload over every argument, caller, operation ID, chain and escrow. Authorized exact successful lookups precede fresh guards, return the original result, and emit/transfer nothing. Conflict/failed operations do not acquire rights. Existence uses kind, including successful expiry of unit0 with result0. Lock/marker/report IDs use their nonzero operation ID, unique in the per-escrow operation namespace; voucher IDs use the exact frozen domain formula.
